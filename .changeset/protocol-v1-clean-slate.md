Protocol v1: a clean-slate redeploy of every contract, at new addresses, with every `version()` back at 1. Nothing is compatible with the previous deployment.

**Terms.** A slot's terms are `TaxTerms { recipient, rateBps (uint16), minRunwaySeconds (uint32) }` and `AppTerms { target, config }`. `SlotInit` is `{ currency, manager, mutableTax, mutableRecipient, mutableApp, taxTerms, appTerms }`; a manager is required exactly when something is mutable.

- `proposeTerms(TaxTerms, AppTerms, uint8 mask)` queues any combination of `TERM_TAX_RATE | TERM_RECIPIENT | TERM_MIN_RUNWAY | TERM_APP`, each allowed only if the slot was created mutable for it (`mutableTax` covers the tax rate and minimum runway). `TERM_SCOPES` is queued only by accepting an app's offer. `cancelTerms(uint8 mask)` clears whichever of those are queued. Everything lands at the next `buy` once `TERMS_DELAY` has passed, or at `applyTerms()` — callable by the occupant while somebody is seated, by anyone while the slot is vacant. `release` and `liquidate` land nothing: a vacated slot keeps the terms it had until the next buyer funds the new ones, so no eviction ever reads an incoming app.
- `setManager(address)` hands over immediately.
- Views: `terms()` (`Terms { taxTerms, appTerms, manifest }`), `pendingTerms()` (`PendingTerms { taxTerms, appTerms, scopes, mask, proposedAt, ripe }`), `taxTerms()`, `appTerms()`, `manifest()`, `grantStatus()`, `mutableTax()`, `mutableRecipient()`, `mutableApp()`, `taxRateBps()`, `minRunwaySeconds()`, `scopes()`, and `getSlotInfo()` with grouped `terms` and `pending`. `Initialized`, `TermsProposed`, `TermsCancelled` and `TermsApplied` carry the structs and mask.

**Apps.** An app declares what it asks of a slot in `manifest(bytes32 config) returns (Manifest { scopes, feeBps, feeRecipient })`: its scopes (callbacks, `onInstall`, and `strict`) as `ScopesLib` bits, and a share of rent. The slot reads the offer when the app is proposed and when it attaches, keeps its own copy, and pays the fee share of collected rent on every payout (`AppFeePaid`). Payouts and callbacks never read the app's current answer.

- An app changing its offer is an offer to accept: `grantStatus()` returns `(accepted, offered, feeDiffers, permissionsDiffer)`.
- The manager takes it with `grant(Manifest expected)`, which reverts `ManifestChanged` unless the app still offers exactly `expected`, and `NothingToAccept` if it would change nothing (`ScopesGranted(offer, feeApplied, permissionsQueued)`).
- A new fee applies at once on any slot, after paying out earned rent under the old fee: it never changes what an occupant pays.
- New scopes queue under `TERM_SCOPES` for the next buy, only when the app is mutable. At that transition the app is read again; scopes it no longer declares are dropped (`ScopesDropped`). A queued `TERM_APP` supersedes them.
- Apps validate configuration in `checkSettings(bytes32)`. One that cannot answer within the stipend the slot reads it under is refused at proposal (`ManifestTooExpensive`), rather than attaching as nothing later.
- `onInstall(SlotContext)` tells an app it has become a slot's app, from `initialize` and wherever a queued app lands. It honours `strict`, and never fires on an eviction. `onUninstall(SlotContext)` tells the outgoing app, before the swap, so it is handed its own settings — capped and swallowed even for a `strict` app, because an app able to refuse its own removal is one a manager can never replace.
- **Discovery.** `IDescribedApp` is one call: `definition()` returns a JSON document whose `config` is a JSON Schema 2020-12, which an application hands to `react-jsonschema-form`, JSONForms or AJV untouched. `AppSchemaLib` builds it from the app's own constants, so a published bound cannot drift from the check that enforces it. The protocol adds `x-abi` (an ordered `AbiParameter[]`, since encoding is positional), `x-config-encoding` (`inline` or `registered`), `x-optional`, and `x-semantic` / `x-unit` / `x-minimum` / `x-maximum` / `x-enum-labels`. Every value is a string, because a `uint64` bound does not survive `JSON.parse` as a number. The old `HookDescriptor` array, its `HookBounds` and its families are gone. One that cannot answer within the stipend the slot reads it under is refused at proposal (`ManifestTooExpensive`), rather than attaching as nothing later.
- `onInstall(SlotContext)` tells an app it has become a slot's app, from `initialize` and wherever a queued app lands. It honours `strict`, and never fires on an eviction. `onUninstall(SlotContext)` tells the outgoing app, before the swap, so it is handed its own settings — capped and swallowed even for a `strict` app, because an app able to refuse its own removal is one a manager can never replace.

**Debt.** Tax an occupant's deposit could not cover is `debtOf(account)` (`DebtRepaid` when paid). It is paid out of the account's next buy of the slot, out of the price when the account is bought out, and out of a top-up before it funds the deposit.

**Hardening.** Settling rounds the paid seconds up, so settling every block charges what settling once does. A currency that delivers less than it transfers reverts with `CurrencyTakesACut`. `OfferBook.acceptOffer(slot, id, minPrice)` pins the lowest accepted price. Debt repaid at a buyout is paid out under the outgoing terms. `minDepositToHold(price)` is the floor under the terms in force; the OfferBook checks a fill against it and counts a bidder's debt when judging an offer fundable. AdLand buy-and-publish approves what the slot will charge, debt included. App re-entry bars are written only by the slot itself (`MinimumTenure.NotTheSlot`), and AdLand records them on release and liquidation for slots with a tenure window.

**Storage.** Every concern lives at its own ERC-7201 location, so each group can grow in an upgrade.

**Indexer.** Slot columns follow the contract: `taxRateBps`, `minRunwaySeconds`, `mutableTax`, `scope*` for accepted scopes, `pendingTaxRateBps`, `pendingHasTaxRate`, `pendingMinRunwaySeconds`, `pendingHasMinRunway`, `pendingScopes`, `pendingHasScopes`. `debtRepaidTotal` on the slot and a `debt_repaid_event` table record every `DebtRepaid`.

**AdLand.** An ordinary app, with no slot entrypoint of its own. `registerAdConfig({ tenureWindow, moderation, key })` stores the configuration and returns its `keccak256`, which is the slot's `bytes32` app config; changing any of it goes through `proposeTerms` like every other app term. `claimKey(slot)` is permissionless. Moderation is `approveCreative`, `rejectCreative` and `moderationOf`, with the mode read from the registered config.

**Carried over.** `SlotFactory.collectAll` / `collectFrom`, `OfferBook`, `SlotCollective` governance (relays masks; `proposeApp` takes `AppTerms`; `grant` relays app offers; `setSplit(current, next, tokens)` pays out under the current split first; `multicall` batches any of its calls under their own role checks), `SlotBoundNFTFactory` (the wrapper beacon is set in `initialize`).

**SDK.** `SlotInit`, `TaxTerms`, `AppTerms`, `NO_APP`, `TERMS`, `ALL_TERMS`; `proposeTerms({ taxRateBps, recipient, minRunwaySeconds, appTerms })`; `cancelTerms(slot, mask)`; `grantStatus(slot)`, `grant(slot, expected)`, the OfferBook (`offerBoard`, `offerAt`, `isOfferFundable`, `offerCost`, `postOffer`, `cancelOffer`, `approveOfferBook`, `authorizeOfferBook`, `acceptOffer(slot, id, minPrice)`), app reads (`readManifest`, `checkSettings`, `appDefinition`, `appSettings`), `liquidateAndBuy` for ERC-20 slots, reads for `terms`, `manifest`, `manager`, `collectedTax`, `lastSettled`, `occupiedSince` and `minRunwaySeconds`, `debtOf(slot, account)`, a `CollectivesClient` (create, tax and app relays with batching, cancels, `sweep`, `distribute`, `setSplit` with payout first, `grant`, `batch` over `multicall`, roles) and `useCollectivesClient`, `Manifest`, `SCOPE_BITS`, `unpackScopes`, `TERMS.SCOPES`; `SlotState` carries `taxRateBps`, `minRunwaySeconds`, the three `mutable*` flags, `app`, `settings`, `manifest` and `scopes`, read from `getSlotInfo()` in one call; `pending` carries `taxTerms`, `appTerms`, `scopes`, `mask` and a flag per term.
