Slot methods · writes

Grouped by who may call, then what the slot calls on its module.

CallerMethodDoesProposed
OccupantThe current holder. selfAssess also takes an operator. selfAssess(newPrice)Restate the price; tax follows from now on. The module may veto.
withdraw(amount)Take deposit back, keeping what the current terms require.
release()Leave. The rest of the deposit is refunded.
setOperator(operator, allowed)Let someone reprice on your behalf, for this tenure only.
applyTerms()Land ripe terms without waiting for a buy. Anyone’s call once the slot is vacant.
Anyone buy(account, selfAssessedPrice, depositAmount, maxPayment)payablePay the sitting price (nothing if vacant) plus a deposit, and seat account. Lands ripe terms; the module may veto.
topUp(amount)payableAdd to the occupant’s deposit. Repays their debt first.
liquidate()Evict an occupant whose deposit is empty. No bounty; the reward is a vacant slot.
collect()Pay out collected tax: the module fee, then the recipient.
claim(account)Send account a payout that could not be pushed to them.
multicall(bytes[] data)Several calls in one transaction, e.g. liquidate then buy.
ManagerOnly on a slot created with something mutable. proposeTerms(taxTerms, moduleTerms, mask)Queue changes; they wait 1 hour. Checks the module’s settings and pins the manifest it reviewed.
cancelTerms(mask)Drop the named queued changes, leaving the rest.
grant(Manifest expected)Accept what the module declares now. A fee change applies at once; new scopes wait for the next buy.grantModuleRequest(reviewedRequest)
setManager(next)Hand the slot to another manager, immediately.
FactoryOnce. initialize(SlotInit)Set governance and terms, check the module’s settings, copy its manifest, call onInstall.
Slot → moduleISlotModule. Only callbacks named in the slot’s scopes are called. checkSettings(bytes settings)viewRevert if the settings are invalid. At proposal and install.validateSettings
manifest(bytes settings)viewThe scopes and fee the module asks for. May depend on the settings. At proposal, install and grant.requestedGrant
beforeBuy · beforeSelfAssess(ctx)viewVeto by reverting. Uncapped gas.
afterBuy · afterRelease · afterLiquidate · afterSettle(ctx)Told after the fact. 500k gas; a revert is ignored unless strict.ctx.owed/paid → taxOwed/taxPaid
onInstall(ctx)Attached. A revert is ignored unless strict.
onUninstall(ctx)Being replaced or removed. Always capped, a revert always ignored.
